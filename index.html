What is a Mortgage-Link Account? (按揭高息存息戶口)

In Hong Kong, major mortgage lenders (such as HSBC, Bank of China, Hang Seng, Standard Chartered) bundle a high-interest deposit account with your mortgage. The interest rate on your eligible deposit equals your mortgage loan rate, effectively offsetting interest payments dollar-for-dollar.

HKMA & Bank Cap: The preferential deposit balance is capped at 50% of your daily outstanding mortgage loan balance. Deposit interest earned is tax-free under the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance.

Hong Kong Mortgage Rules, Stamp Duty & Policies Summary

Current regulatory reference guide based on official HKMA circulars, GovHK / IRD Stamp Duty Ordinance (effective 26 February 2026), and HKMC guidelines.

1. HKMA Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

Per the HKMA Countercyclical Macroprudential Measures effective 16 October 2024:

  • Standardized Bank Cap: 70% LTV across all residential properties regardless of property value or self-use status.
  • Applicable to both income-assessed and net-worth-assessed mortgage borrowers.
  • Non-residential properties (offices, industrial, shops) standardized at 70% LTV.
  • Bank Valuation Rule: Banks calculate the maximum loan off the lower of the purchase price or the bank valuation; any valuation shortfall must be paid upfront in cash.

2. Mortgage Insurance Programme (HKMC MIP)

Allows eligible owner-occupiers to borrow above the 70% HKMA bank ceiling:

  • Up to 90% LTV: Completed residential properties up to HK$ 10,000,000 for first-time buyers.
  • Up to 80% LTV: Properties between HK$ 10,000,000 and HK$ 15,000,000.
  • Properties up to HK$ 30,000,000 eligible under the sliding scale.
  • Single premium can be financed directly into the mortgage loan; banks typically offer an approximate 35% discount.

3. Ad Valorem Stamp Duty (AVD — Effective 26 Feb 2026)

Per GovHK / Inland Revenue Department (Part 1 of Scale 1 & Scale 2 for residential; Scale 3 for non-residential). Charged on the higher of consideration or market value:

  • Does not exceed HK$ 4,000,000: Flat HK$ 100.
  • HK$ 4,000,001 – HK$ 4,323,780: HK$ 100 + 20% of excess over HK$ 4M.
  • HK$ 4,323,781 – HK$ 4,500,000: 1.50%.
  • HK$ 4,500,001 – HK$ 4,935,480: HK$ 67,500 + 10% of excess over HK$ 4.5M.
  • HK$ 4,935,481 – HK$ 6,000,000: 2.25%.
  • HK$ 6,000,001 – HK$ 6,642,860: HK$ 135,000 + 10% of excess over HK$ 6M.
  • HK$ 6,642,861 – HK$ 9,000,000: 3.00%.
  • HK$ 9,000,001 – HK$ 10,080,000: HK$ 270,000 + 10% of excess over HK$ 9M.
  • HK$ 10,080,001 – HK$ 20,000,000: 3.75%.
  • HK$ 20,000,001 – HK$ 21,739,120: HK$ 750,000 + 10% of excess over HK$ 20M.
  • HK$ 21,739,121 – HK$ 100,000,000: 4.25% (Non-residential Scale 3 caps at 4.25%).
  • HK$ 100,000,001 – HK$ 109,574,470 (Residential): HK$ 4,250,000 + 30% of excess over HK$ 100M.
  • Exceeds HK$ 109,574,470 (Residential): 6.50%.

4. DSR, Rates, Rent & Lock-In Penalty

  • DSR Cap: 50% standardized across self-use and letting properties.
  • Government Rates (Rating Ordinance): 5% of estimated rateable annual rental value (billed quarterly).
  • Government Rent (Government Rent Ordinance): 3% of rateable value (applies to New Territories and post-1997 leases).
  • Bank Cash Rebates (HKMA Rule): If greater than 1%, the entire rebate must be deducted from the loan amount for LTV calculation.
  • Early Redemption Lock-In: Banks typically enforce a 2-year lock-in period (Year 1: ~2% penalty + rebate clawback; Year 2: ~1% penalty).
