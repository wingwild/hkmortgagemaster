What is a Mortgage-Link Account? (按揭高息存息戶口)

In Hong Kong, major mortgage lenders (such as HSBC, Bank of China, Hang Seng, Standard Chartered) bundle a high-interest deposit account with your mortgage. The interest rate on your eligible deposit equals your mortgage loan rate (e.g., 3.625%), effectively offsetting interest payments dollar-for-dollar.

★ HKMA & Bank Cap: The preferential deposit balance is capped at 50% of your daily outstanding mortgage loan balance. Interest earned is completely tax-free in HK.

Hong Kong Mortgage Rules, Stamp Duty & Policies Summary

Current regulatory reference guide based on official HKMA, IRD, and HKMC guidelines

🏛️ 1. HKMA Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

Following the adjustments announced on 16 October 2024:

  • Standardized Bank Cap: 70% LTV across all residential properties regardless of property value or whether self-occupied.
  • Applicable to both income-assessed and net-worth-assessed mortgage borrowers.
  • Non-residential properties (offices, industrial, shops) standardized at 70% LTV.
  • The 10% penalty reduction for borrowers with existing mortgages was fully lifted.

🛡️ 2. Mortgage Insurance Programme (HKMC MIP)

Allows owner-occupiers to borrow above the 70% bank ceiling:

  • Up to 90% LTV: Properties with values up to HK$ 10,000,000 for first-time home buyers.
  • Up to 80% LTV: Properties with values between HK$ 10,000,000 and HK$ 15,000,000.
  • Properties up to HK$ 30,000,000 eligible under sliding scale.
  • Single premium can be financed directly into the mortgage loan.
  • Participating banks routinely offer an approximate 35% discount on the standard MIP premium.

📑 3. Ad Valorem Stamp Duty (AVD Scale 2)

Following the 2024 Budget repeal of Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD), New Residential Stamp Duty (NRSD), and Special Stamp Duty (SSD), Scale 2 rates apply:

  • Up to HK$ 3,000,000: Flat HK$ 100.
  • HK$ 3,000,001 - HK$ 3,528,240: HK$ 100 + 10% of excess over HK$ 3M.
  • HK$ 3,528,241 - HK$ 4,500,000: 1.50%.
  • HK$ 4,500,001 - HK$ 5,316,000: HK$ 67,500 + 10% of excess over HK$ 4.5M.
  • HK$ 5,316,001 - HK$ 7,200,000: 2.25%.
  • HK$ 7,200,001 - HK$ 8,294,030: HK$ 162,000 + 10% of excess over HK$ 7.2M.
  • HK$ 8,294,031 - HK$ 10,000,000: 3.00%.
  • HK$ 10,000,001 - HK$ 10,757,400: HK$ 300,000 + 10% of excess over HK$ 10M.
  • HK$ 10,757,401 - HK$ 20,000,000: 3.75%.
  • Over HK$ 21,739,120: 4.25%.

⚖️ 4. DSR, Rates, Rent & Lock-In Penalty

  • DSR Cap: 50% standardized across self-use and letting properties.
  • Government Rates: 5% of estimated rateable annual rental value (billed quarterly).
  • Government Rent: 3% of rateable value (applies to properties in New Territories and post-1997 leases).
  • Bank Cash Rebates: If greater than 1%, HKMA mandates that the rebate amount must be accounted for by deducting from the mortgage loan amount.
  • Early Redemption Penalty: Hong Kong banks enforce a 1 to 2-year lock-in period. Prepayment within year 1 incurs ~1-2% penalty plus rebate clawback.
