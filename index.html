What is a Mortgage-Link Account? (按揭高息存息戶口)

In Hong Kong, major mortgage lenders (such as HSBC, Bank of China, Hang Seng, Standard Chartered) bundle a high-interest deposit account with your mortgage. The interest rate on your eligible deposit equals your mortgage loan rate, effectively offsetting interest payments dollar-for-dollar. HKMA & Bank Cap: Preferential deposit balance is capped at 50% of your daily outstanding mortgage loan balance. Deposit interest is tax-free under the Hong Kong Inland Revenue Ordinance.

Hong Kong Mortgage Rules, Stamp Duty & Policies Summary

Current regulatory reference guide based on official HKMA circulars, GovHK / IRD Stamp Duty Ordinance (effective 26 Feb 2026), and HKMC guidelines.

1. HKMA Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

Per the HKMA Countercyclical Macroprudential Measures effective 16 October 2024:

  • Standardized Bank Cap: 70% LTV across all residential properties regardless of value or self-use status.
  • Applicable to both income-assessed and net-worth-assessed mortgage borrowers.
  • Non-residential properties (offices, industrial, shops) standardized at 70% LTV.
  • Bank Valuation Rule: Banks calculate loans off the lower of purchase price or bank valuation; any shortfall is added to upfront cash.

2. Mortgage Insurance Programme (HKMC MIP)

Allows eligible owner-occupiers to borrow above the 70% HKMA bank ceiling:

  • Up to 90% LTV: Completed residential properties up to HK$ 10,000,000 for first-time buyers.
  • Up to 80% LTV: Properties between HK$ 10,000,000 and HK$ 15,000,000.
  • Properties up to HK$ 30,000,000 eligible under the sliding scale.
  • Single premium can be financed into the loan; banks routinely offer an approximate 35% discount.

3. Ad Valorem Stamp Duty (AVD — Effective 26 Feb 2026)

Per GovHK / Inland Revenue Department (Part 1 of Scale 1 & Scale 2 for residential; Scale 3 for non-residential). Charged on the higher of consideration or market value:

  • ≤ HK$ 4,000,000: HK$ 100
  • HK$ 4,000,001 – HK$ 4,323,780: HK$ 100 + 20% (>$4M)
  • HK$ 4,323,781 – HK$ 4,500,000: 1.50%
  • HK$ 4,500,001 – HK$ 4,935,480: HK$ 67,500 + 10% (>$4.5M)
  • HK$ 4,935,481 – HK$ 6,000,000: 2.25%
  • HK$ 6,000,001 – HK$ 6,642,860: HK$ 135,000 + 10% (>$6M)
  • HK$ 6,642,861 – HK$ 9,000,000: 3.00%
  • HK$ 9,000,001 – HK$ 10,080,000: HK$ 270,000 + 10% (>$9M)
  • HK$ 10,080,001 – HK$ 20,000,000: 3.75%
  • HK$ 20,000,001 – HK$ 21,739,120: HK$ 750,000 + 10% (>$20M)
  • HK$ 21,739,121 – HK$ 100,000,000: 4.25% (Scale 3 caps at 4.25%)
  • HK$ 100,000,001 – HK$ 109,574,470 (Res): HK$ 4,250,000 + 30% (>$100M)
  • > HK$ 109,574,470 (Res): 6.50%

4. DSR, Rates, Rent & Lock-In Penalty

  • DSR Cap: 50% standardized across self-use and letting properties.
  • Government Rates (Rating Ordinance): 5% of estimated rateable annual rental value (billed quarterly).
  • Government Rent (Government Rent Ordinance): 3% of rateable value (New Territories & post-1997 leases).
  • Bank Cash Rebates (HKMA Rule): If greater than 1%, the entire rebate must be deducted from the loan amount for LTV calculation.
  • Early Redemption Lock-In: Banks typically enforce a 2-year lock-in period (Year 1: ~2% penalty + rebate clawback; Year 2: ~1% penalty).

Core Mathematical Formulas

  • Bank Valuation & Shortfall: Loan Basis = min(Property Price, Bank Valuation); Valuation Shortfall = max(0, Property Price − Bank Valuation) added directly to upfront cash.
  • Monthly & Bi-Weekly Amortization: Standard level-payment formula M = P·[r(1+r)ⁿ] / [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]. Bi-weekly simulates 26 half-payments per year (r = annualRate / 26) to compute exact years cut and interest saved.
  • Sell-On Break-Even Price: Solves S·(1 − sellComm) − sellLegal = PurchasePrice + BuyingCosts + CumulativeInterest + HoldingCosts + Renovation + LockInPenalty − RentOffset.
